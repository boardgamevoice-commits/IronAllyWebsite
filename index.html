Frequently Asked Questions常见问题解答

Does IronAlly work offline in basement gyms without Wi-Fi or cell service?

Yes. All core workout logging, 357-exercise anatomy guides, rest timers, plate calculator, 1RM tracking, and fatigue heatmaps run 100% locally on your iPhone or iPad with no account required. Only optional on-demand AI Coach features require an internet connection.

How is IronAlly different from Strong or Hevy?

IronAlly eliminates redundant set-completion checkmarks so planned sets are editable immediately, includes an interactive 36+ muscle anatomy fatigue heatmap (72h large / 36h small muscle recovery model), calculates daily CNS recovery readiness from Apple Health HRV and sleep stages, and offers on-demand AI load calibration, equipment/meal camera scans, and video form analysis.

Where is my Apple Health and workout data stored?

Your workout history, custom templates, progress photos (Face ID protected), and Apple Health metrics stay strictly on your device in local storage. IronAlly has zero third-party ads, zero tracking SDKs, never sells user data, and supports full JSON backup/restore and CSV import from Strong, Hevy, and Xunji.

Is IronAlly a medical device?

No. IronAlly is a strength training and general wellness tracking app designed for healthy adults. Its CNS readiness score, fatigue heatmap, and AI suggestions are for fitness guidance only and do not provide medical diagnoses, rehabilitation prescriptions, or clinical advice.

How much does IronAlly cost and which languages are supported?

Core workout logging, the 357-exercise anatomy library, fatigue heatmaps, rest timers, Live Activities, and JSON/CSV backups are free. IronAlly Pro ($6.99/month or $49.99/year with a 7-day free trial) unlocks AI Coach features. The app natively supports 9 languages: English, Simplified & Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, and Portuguese.
